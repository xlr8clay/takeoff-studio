Labor and equipment = quantity / crew production x crew cost per hour. Equipment rates are taken as operated; laborers are priced separately in each crew. Quantities follow the takeoff: type a quantity to override it, clear it to go back. Trucking is priced in its own table and carried into the total as one line. Bid price = direct cost plus overhead, then markup on top. Equipment, crews and the overhead and markup percents are saved in this browser for every job; quantities, crew choices, material prices and trucking are saved per job. Nothing here is shared with other people who open the page.
